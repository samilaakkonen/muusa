Muusa

AI chord & melody ideas, on your device.

Stuck on a blank track? Muusa sketches a chord progression and melody in seconds, so you can capture an idea while it's fresh and carry it straight into your DAW.

Type in whatever you have in mind, and Muusa generates a musical starting point — chords first, then a melody that fits them. Hear it play back instantly, then export the whole idea as a MIDI file to finish in Logic, Ableton, GarageBand, or wherever you produce.

Built on Apple Intelligence

Muusa runs entirely on the on-device model at the core of Apple Intelligence — no account, no subscription, no cloud. Your ideas never leave your device, it works offline, and there are no usage limits or extra costs.

Made to fit your workflow

  • Generate chord progressions and melodies from a simple prompt
  • Preview any idea instantly with built-in playback
  • Export to MIDI — on Mac, just drag and drop into your DAW
  • Clean, focused, distraction-free design

Muusa isn't here to write your song. It's here to break the blank page — a fast source of inspiration you shape into something of your own.

Before you buy

Muusa requires Apple Intelligence, available on supported devices. Before purchasing, please make sure your device supports Apple Intelligence and that it's turned on in Settings.
